## 1. About This App

**CISC Kids** is a mobile reading app for **Grade 1–3 learners**. A student reads words and short passages out loud, and the app listens through the microphone, checks how accurately the words were read, and gives instant feedback.

The app has **three kinds of users**:

| User | What they do |
|------|--------------|
| **Student** | Practices reading and views their own progress. |
| **Teacher (Faculty)** | Creates classes, approves students, and reviews reading results. |
| **Admin** | Manages all users and classes in the system. |

---

## 2. Before You Begin

To use the app smoothly, make sure you have:

- **A phone or tablet** with the CISC Kids app installed.
- **An internet connection** (the app needs it to check reading and save results).
- **Microphone permission turned ON** (the app records the student reading aloud).
- **A working email address** (needed to create an account).

> 💡 The first time a student records, the phone may ask "Allow microphone?" — tap **Allow**.

## 3. Getting Started

### 3.1 Signing In

1. Open the app. You will see the **Login** screen.
2. Type your **Email Address** and **Password**.
3. Tap the **eye icon** if you want to check your password as you type.
4. Tap **Login**.

> ⚠️ If you type the wrong password too many times, the app pauses logins for a few seconds for safety. Just wait for the timer and try again.

### 3.2 Creating a New Account

If you don't have an account yet:

1. On the Login screen, tap **Sign Up with Email**.
2. **Choose your role** — tap **I am a Student** or **I am a Teacher**.
3. **Create your account (Step 1):**
   - Enter your **email** and a **password** (at least 8 characters), then confirm the password.
   - **Students:** a parent or guardian must tick the **consent checkbox** to continue.
   - Tap **Next**.
4. **Add your details (Step 2):**
   - Add a **profile picture** (optional) — take a photo or choose from the gallery.
   - Enter **First Name**, **Last Name** (Middle Name is optional).
   - Choose your **Gender** and **Date of Birth**.
   - Choose your **Grade Level**.
   - Tap **Complete Registration**.
5. A success animation appears, then a **Welcome** screen. Tap **Start Learning** to enter the app.

### 3.3 The Menu, About, and Logout

- Most screens have a **menu button** (the three lines ☰, or a side menu for Admin).
- Tap it to open **About** (app and team information) or **Logout**.
- Tapping **Logout** asks you to confirm before signing you out.

## 4. Student Module

When a student logs in, they land on the **Home** screen with a friendly greeting and four buttons.

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Start Reading** | Begin a reading practice. |
| **Reading Performance** | See your progress and past results. |
| **My Class** | Join or view your class. |
| **My Profile** | View and edit your details. |

### 4.1 Start Reading

1. Tap **Start Reading**.
2. Choose a tab at the top:
   - **Words** — practice single words, grouped into **Chapters** and **Lessons**.
   - **Passages** — practice reading a full short story.
3. **For Words:** tap a **Chapter**, then tap a **word** to practice it.
   **For Passages:** tap the **passage** you want to read.

#### Doing a Reading Activity

1. The word or passage appears on screen.
2. Tap the **microphone button** and read out loud.
3. Tap the microphone again to **stop**. The app shows *"Please wait a moment"* while it checks your reading.
4. You'll get **feedback**:
   - **Words:** "Great job!" if correct, or "Try again" if not.
   - **Passages:** an **accuracy score** and a list of any **miscues** (reading mistakes).
5. Use the buttons to **Try Again**, go to the **Previous (‹)** or **Next (›)** item.

> ⭐ A green **check mark (✓)** appears on words you have read correctly, so you can see what you've finished.

### 4.2 Reading Performance

This screen has four tabs:

| Tab | What you see |
|-----|--------------|
| **Progress** | How much of the reading materials you've completed. |
| **Sessions** | The words you've mastered. |
| **Analytics** | Charts of your accuracy and common mistakes over time. |
| **History** | A list of every reading session, grouped by passage. |

- In **History**, use the **Week / Month / Year** buttons and the **‹ ›** arrows to change the time period.
- Tap a passage to expand it and see each attempt's **accuracy, words-per-minute, duration,** and **miscue breakdown**.

### 4.3 My Class

- If you are **not in a class yet**, tap **Join a Class**, enter the **class code** your teacher gave you, and tap **Join**.
- After joining, you'll see **"Waiting for Approval"** until your teacher accepts you. You can **Cancel Request** if needed.
- Once approved, the class card shows the **class name, grade level, academic year,** and **class code**.
- To leave, scroll down and tap **Leave Class** (you may be asked for your password to confirm).

> 🔒 A student can only be in **one class at a time**.

### 4.4 My Profile

- Shows your **photo, name,** and **basic information**.
- Tap **Edit** to change your name, birthdate, or sex, then tap **Save**.

## 5. Faculty (Teacher) Module

Teachers use a **bottom tab bar** with four tabs: **Dashboard · My Class · Archive · Profile**.

### 5.1 Dashboard

The Dashboard gives an overview of your classes and how students are reading.

1. At the top, use the **Academic Year** and **Section** filters to choose which class to view.
2. Scroll down to see charts, each with a simple title:

| Section | What it tells you |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Dashboard Overview** | Total number of classes and students. |
| **Gender Distribution** | Boys vs girls in the class. |
| **Class Participation** | How many students are actually reading. |
| **Accuracy Trends** | Whether reading accuracy is improving over time. |
| **Reading Activity Calendar** | Which days students read most. |
| **Miscue Analysis** | The most common reading mistakes and tricky words. |

### 5.2 My Class

This tab lists all your **active classes**.

**To create a class:**
1. Tap **Create New Class**.
2. Enter a **Class Name** (e.g., "Grade 1 Section A") and choose the **Grade Level**.
3. Tap **Create Class**. The app generates a **class code** — share this code with your students so they can join.

**To manage a class:** tap the **three-dot (⋮) icon** on a class card to:
- **Edit Class** — rename it.
- **Archive Class** — move it out of the way (it goes to the Archive tab).
- **Delete Class** — remove it permanently (cannot be undone).

**To view a class's students:** tap the class card.

#### Approving Students & Viewing Their Data

Inside a class (the **My Students** screen):

1. **Pending Requests** appear in a yellow box at the top. Tap **Accept** to add a student, or **Reject** to decline.
2. Use the **search bar** to find a student by name.
3. Tap a student to open their **reading data** — the same Progress / Sessions / Analytics / History tabs students see.
4. On the student's data screen you can **Export to PDF or Excel** to download a report.
5. To remove a student, tap the **three-dot (⋮)** on their row and confirm.

### 5.3 Archive

- Shows classes you've archived.
- Tap the **three-dot (⋮)** on a class to **Unarchive** (restore it) or **Delete** it.

### 5.4 Profile

- Shows your teacher details. Tap **Edit** to update them and **Save**.

## 6. Admin Module

Admins use a **side menu** (open it with the menu button ☰). The menu has: **Dashboard · User Management · Class Management · About**.

### 6.1 Dashboard

- Shows **KPI cards** (total students, teachers, active classes) for the current school year.
- Use the **Academic Year** dropdown to change the time period for the charts.
- Charts include **Users by Role, Users Registered, Class Status, Classes/Students per Grade, Reading Level Distribution,** and **Gender Distribution**.

### 6.2 User Management

1. **Filter** users by role (Student, Teacher, Admin) or **search** by name.
2. Tap **Add User** to create a new account (fill in email, password, role, and personal details).
3. Tap a user to **view or edit** their details.
4. From the edit screen you can **update**, **delete**, or **send a password-reset email** to that user.

### 6.3 Class Management

1. Use the **Academic Year** dropdown to choose the school year.
2. See **all classes** in the system for that year.
3. Tap **Create Class** to make a class and **assign it to a teacher**.
4. Tap the **three-dot (⋮)** on a class to **rename, archive, unarchive,** or **delete** it.
5. Tap a class to open its **dashboard** (reading charts for that class).

### 6.4 Viewing Faculty Data

- From class or user screens, an admin can open a **faculty member's data** to see their classes, students, and reading statistics — useful for monitoring teachers' classes.

## 7. Tips & Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| **The microphone doesn't record** | Make sure you tapped **Allow** for microphone permission. Check it in your phone's app settings. |
| **"Network Error" while checking reading** | Check your internet connection, then tap **Try Again**. |
| **"Connection timed out"** | Your internet may be slow. Move to a stronger signal and retry. |
| **Can't log in after many tries** | Wait for the on-screen timer to finish, then try again. |
| **Student can't join a class** | Double-check the **class code** (letters and numbers must match exactly). Remember a student can only be in one class. |
| **Join request not accepted** | The teacher must **Accept** the request in **My Class → My Students**. |
| **Forgot password** | A teacher account can ask the admin to send a **password-reset email**. |

## 8. Glossary of Terms

| Term | Simple meaning |
|------|----------------|
| **Miscue** | A reading mistake. The app finds four kinds: see below. |
| **Substitution** | Said a different word than the one written. |
| **Omission** | Skipped a word. |
| **Insertion** | Added a word that wasn't there. |
| **Repetition** | Repeated a word. |
| **Accuracy** | The percentage of words read correctly. |
| **WPM (Words Per Minute)** | How fast the student reads. |
| **Class Code** | A short code students type to join a teacher's class. |
| **Pending / Approval** | A student has asked to join a class and is waiting for the teacher to accept. |
| **Archive** | To set a class aside without deleting it; it can be restored later. |
